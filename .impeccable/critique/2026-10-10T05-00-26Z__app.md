## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM: authored for this product. Paper/ink, single vermilion anchor, pen-tool motif used with meaning (QC anchors, selection handles, progress anchor), domain-native copy. Weakness is structural: a sidebar + stacked form shell that collapses to a hamburger; the phone is a shrunk desktop.

Deterministic scan: `impeccable detect app components` = 0 findings (1 known `broken-image` false positive ignored in config). Browser overlay injected on /generate, /aset, /ekspor, /pengaturan, /riset (desktop) and /generate, /aset (mobile): only sidebar email truncation (intentional, has title), account chip nested-card, nested "Lanjutan" details, ProgressLine width transition, Sonner toast height transition and the detector's own glow (false positives). Detector missed the /aset phone overflow; manual measurement caught it.
